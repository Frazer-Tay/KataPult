# KataPult Practice Companion Walkthrough Notes

## Slide 1: KataPult: Bahasa Indo Practice Companion
Subtitle: A working prototype for short, structured Bahasa Indo revision.
Screenshot: Homepage.
Speaker notes: Position KataPult as a working internal prototype. The useful claim is modest: it brings vocabulary, imbuhan, persamaan, writing support and repeat practice into one place.

## Slide 2: Preparation Problem
Headline: The issue was not lack of material. It was restart friction.
Speaker notes: The pain is not that learners have no content. The pain is that content is scattered, weak areas are hard to isolate, and restarting revision requires too much decision-making.

## Slide 3: What KataPult Does
Headline: KataPult organises practice around learning jobs.
Screenshot: Homepage.
Speaker notes: Daily Challenge gives a restart point. Targeted drills cover vocabulary, imbuhan, Persamaan MCQ and Persamaan Latihan. Writing support covers Karangan, Flashcards and Surat Resmi. Custom Test currently supports imbuhan and persamaan.

## Slide 4: Practice Loop
Headline: Pick a weak area, practise briefly, return later.
Screenshot: Daily Challenge.
Speaker notes: The loop is designed to reduce restart friction: choose a module, complete a short drill, review feedback or mistakes, then return later. Do not claim measured learning outcomes yet; describe it as a designed workflow.

## Slide 5: Feature Walkthrough
Headline: Each component has a specific role.
Screenshot: Persamaan view.
Speaker notes: Vocabulary supports browse mode, SRS review, examples, translations and XP. Imbuhan uses fill-in blanks, root prompts, hints, explanations, saved progress and mistake review. Persamaan MCQ focuses on contextual synonym recognition. Persamaan Latihan moves to typed recall. Karangan supports essay vocabulary through definition practice and synonyms. Flashcards support essay planning and phrase recall. Surat Resmi provides navigable formal-letter scaffolds. Custom Test currently covers imbuhan and persamaan with 5 to 50 questions.

## Slide 6: Current Scale And Early Use
Headline: Current scale: enough content to justify refinement.
Screenshot: Daily Challenge.
Speaker notes: Use numbers carefully. 1,554 active drill-ready items and 971 current custom-test items show useful coverage. 40+ early users and 4+ teams reached are early-reach indicators, not evidence of learning efficacy.

## Slide 7: Next Refinement Steps
Headline: Structured enough to use now, clear enough to improve next.
Speaker notes: The responsible next step is content quality review, usage analysis and focused product refinement. Generated or expanded items should be reviewed before being treated as validated. Analytics can support future review of visits, section time, completion patterns and return behaviour.
